01 Option pricing: Monte Carlo vs. closed formSimulated geometric Brownian motion to price European and Asian options, checked against Black-Scholes where a closed form exists. active +

Implemented plain Monte Carlo, then antithetic variates and control variates to bring the variance down. The Asian option has no closed form, so the European one serves as a sanity check on the same paths. Convergence plots included.

Python · Stochastic processes · Monte Carlo · Quantitative finance
Code on GitHub ↗
02 Volatility modelling with GARCHFitting GARCH(1,1) and extensions to daily index returns and evaluating value-at-risk forecasts. active +

Compared a GARCH(1,1) with normal and Student-t innovations, plus a GJR-GARCH for the leverage effect. Backtested one-day-ahead value-at-risk with Kupiec and Christoffersen tests. The t-distribution matters more than the asymmetry, at least on this data.

R · Time series · GARCH · Risk
03 Linear regression from first principlesOLS, the Gauss-Markov theorem and its failure modes, derived by hand and verified in code. completed +

Wrote up the projection-matrix view of OLS, proved unbiasedness and the variance formula, then built the estimator in NumPy without calling any regression library. Simulations show exactly where the theorem stops holding once errors are heteroskedastic or correlated.

Python · LaTeX · Linear algebra · Statistics

04 Markov chain model of customer churnDiscrete-time Markov chain fitted to subscription data, with stationary distribution and expected lifetime. completed +

Estimated the transition matrix from monthly states (active, paused, churned), derived the absorption probabilities and expected time to churn analytically, then checked them against simulation. Course project for stochastic processes.

Python · Markov chains · Stochastic processes
