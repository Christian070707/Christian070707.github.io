Projects

01 Forecasting Dutch inflationARIMA and VAR models on CBS and ECB data, evaluated out-of-sample against a random walk benchmark. active +

Monthly HICP data from 2000 onward. Compared univariate ARIMA against a small VAR with energy prices and unemployment, using rolling-window forecasts and Diebold-Mariano tests. The VAR helps at short horizons, but not by much beyond six months.

R · Time series · ARIMA · VAR · Forecast evaluation

02 House prices and interest ratesPanel regression of regional house prices on mortgage rates, income and supply across Dutch municipalities. active +

Fixed-effects and first-difference models on municipality-level panel data, with clustered standard errors. Most of the work was in cleaning and merging the sources, then checking whether the results survive a change in specification.

Stata · Panel data · Fixed effects · Data cleaning
03 Did the minimum wage increase reduce hours?Difference-in-differences evaluation of a policy change, as a course project. completed +

Used a difference-in-differences design with an event-study plot to check parallel trends, plus a placebo test on an earlier period. Written up as a short paper with a replication script.

R · Causal inference · Difference-in-differences · Policy evaluation
04 Portfolio optimisationMarkowitz mean-variance portfolios with a shrinkage covariance estimator, backtested on European equities. completed +

Compared the sample covariance with Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage and an equal-weight benchmark. The interesting result was how unstable the unconstrained weights are, and how much a simple no-short constraint fixes.

Python · Operations research · Optimisation · Finance
